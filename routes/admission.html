Admission

NCST admission is built to be simple and supportive. From your first inquiry to enrollment confirmation, you will get clear requirements, quick evaluation, and help choosing the campus and program that fit your goals.

  • Guided consultations and campus tours
  • Fast document evaluation and advising
  • Enrollment support with flexible payment options

How It Works

Admission process in four clear steps

01

Create Your Account

Register through the admission portal and complete your basic profile details.

02

Submit Requirements

Upload your documents online or submit copies to the Admissions Office.

03

Evaluation and Payment

Wait for assessment results and follow enrollment payment instructions.

04

Confirm Enrollment

Receive confirmation, activate your student account, and prepare for classes.

Frequently Asked

Quick answers for applicants

Is there an entrance exam for SHS applicants?

No entrance exam is required for incoming Senior High School applicants.

Can I submit requirements online?

Yes. You may upload your documents through the admission portal for initial evaluation.

When do I receive enrollment confirmation?

After your documents are evaluated and payment is verified, confirmation is sent through your portal account.
