Historical Background

The National College of Science & Technology (NCST) was born in the historic and progressive town of Dasmariñas, Cavite in 1998. As a brain child of a 23-year old visionary entrepreneur in the person of Emerson B. Atanacio, NCST has slowly yet steadily carved its niche in the fast growing and very competitive educational arena not only in the province but in the entire CALABARZON region as well.

With an initial enrollment of 550 students in 1998, the school has now increased ten times from its original number as it now has a total population of 11,000 students in all its regular programs.

The school has been faithful in its mission to provide industry responsive graduates, thus in 2005 it has ventured into its "first institutionally developed" course offering which is the Certificate in Manufacturing Technology and the Diploma in Production Technology course which is under the Dual Training Systems Department.

These courses both apply the dual training modality where the students are being trained in two areas of learning—the school and the industry. With an initial enrollee of 10 students in November of 2005, the DTS department has now reached a whooping figure of more than 1,000 students within its first year of operation.

Our Core Values

MAKATAO

Compassion for Others

This value promotes a deep understanding of students' welfare as NCST's primary concern. It ensures a caring, inclusive, and supportive environment by providing safe spaces, responsive services, and learning amenities conducive to academic excellence, personal growth, and holistic development. It fosters respect, compassion, and collaboration, empowering students to reach their full potential.

MAKABAYAN

Nationalistic

This value seeks to kindle the nationalistic spirit of the people. It instills in the NCST community a strong sense of mission, pride, and responsibility to serve the nation by excelling in their skills, knowledge, and abilities. Through the promotion of science and technology, it encourages innovation, excellence, and active participation in nation-building, contributing to the country's sustainable growth and development.

MAKADIYOS

God-fearing

The foremost value that seeks to nurture and sustain a lasting relationship with God. It inspires the NCST community to live with courage, integrity, and compassion, drawing strength and confidence from faith, love, and a deep commitment to God Almighty. This value guides moral decision-making, promotes spiritual growth, and encourages a life of service grounded in humility, gratitude, and respect for others.

MAKAKALIKASAN

Responsible Stewardship

This value is anchored on NCST’s ideal of having a high regard for the preservation and conservation of the country’s environment and natural resources. It promotes environmental awareness, responsibility, and sustainable practices within the academic community, encouraging active participation in protecting ecological balance and fostering a culture of stewardship for present and future generations
